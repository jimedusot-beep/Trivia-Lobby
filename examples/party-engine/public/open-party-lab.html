Upstream player caps are not 20-phone performance certification. No gaming stream is needed.

Play the whole collection locally

  1. On a Windows laptop, download the official Open Party Lab Windows ZIP. Unzip the complete archive and launch its EXE.
  2. Keep the laptop and phones on the same Wi-Fi. The laptop displays the game board and a QR join code.
  3. Choose one of the included games, and have family members scan its QR code on their phones. Respect that game's individual player limit.

Advanced users can run from source with Node.js 20+ and `npm run games:clone-all`. Raspberry Pi performance and all-games 20-player support have not been established.

Use the address displayed by your own Open Party Lab launcher. This does not connect rooms or scores.
